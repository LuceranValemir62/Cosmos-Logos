
# 💻 Computer Science

Computer science is the study of computation, algorithms, data structures, information, and intelligent systems.

This folder contains notes about programming, algorithms, systems, machine learning, and the theoretical foundations of computation.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to understand how computation works and how to build useful, correct, and efficient systems.

Computer science connects deeply with:

- Mathematics
- Statistics
- Logic
- Philosophy of mind
- Artificial intelligence
- Cosmological simulations
