# 🔗 Connections

This folder contains essays and notes that connect different fields together.

The most powerful ideas often appear at the boundary between disciplines.

This section explores how mathematics, computer science, philosophy, statistics, and cosmology relate to one another.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this folder is to build deep understanding across subjects.

Instead of studying each field separately, this folder asks:

- How does mathematics describe the universe?
- How does logic connect philosophy and computer science?
- How does statistics connect evidence and cosmology?
- How does computation help us understand reality?
- How does philosophy shape scientific interpretation?

---

## Main Connection Themes

### 1. Mathematics and Cosmology

Ideas:

- General relativity uses differential geometry
- Cosmological models use equations
- The shape of the universe involves topology
- Quantum gravity may require new mathematics

### 2. Logic, Philosophy, and Computer Science

Ideas:

- Formal logic led to computation
- Turing machines connect philosophy and computer science
- Artificial intelligence raises questions about mind
- Proof theory connects mathematics and computation

### 3. Statistics and Scientific Knowledge

Ideas:

- Scientific evidence depends on probability
- Bayesian reasoning connects belief and evidence
- Cosmological data requires statistical inference
- Measurement uncertainty shapes scientific claims

### 4. Philosophy and Cosmology

Ideas:

- What does it mean for the universe to begin?
- Is time fundamental?
- Are scientific models real?
- Can we know what happened before the Big Bang?

### 5. Computation and the Universe

Ideas:

- Is the universe computational?
- Can physical laws be seen as algorithms?
- Are simulations scientific tools or possible realities?
- What is information?
