# 🔭 Cosmology

Cosmology is the study of the universe as a whole: its origin, structure, evolution, and ultimate fate.

This folder contains notes on the Big Bang, dark matter, dark energy, general relativity, galaxies, cosmic expansion, and the deep structure of spacetime.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to understand the universe using physics, mathematics, statistics, and philosophy.

Cosmology asks questions such as:

- How did the universe begin?
- What is space?
- What is time?
- Why does the universe expand?
- What is dark matter?
- What is dark energy?
- Did the universe have a beginning?
- Is the universe finite or infinite?
