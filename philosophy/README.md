
# 🧠 Philosophy

Philosophy is the study of fundamental questions about knowledge, reality, logic, mind, ethics, science, and meaning.

This folder contains philosophical notes, arguments, definitions, and reflections that connect with mathematics, computer science, statistics, and cosmology.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to develop clear thinking, strong argumentation, and deep reflection.

Philosophy helps us ask questions such as:

- What is knowledge?
- What is real?
- What is consciousness?
- What makes an argument valid?
- What is scientific truth?
- Do numbers exist independently of humans?
- Can machines think?

---

## Main Topics

### 1. Logic

Topics:

- Propositional logic
- Predicate logic
- Validity
- Soundness
- Fallacies
- Formal systems

### 2. Epistemology

Epistemology is the study of knowledge.

Topics:

- What is knowledge?
- Justification
- Skepticism
- Rationalism
- Empiricism
- Scientific knowledge

### 3. Metaphysics

Metaphysics is the study of reality.

Topics:

- Existence
- Identity
- Time
- Space
- Causation
- Free will
- Possible worlds

### 4. Philosophy of Mind

Topics:

- Consciousness
- Intelligence
- Artificial intelligence
- Mind-body problem
- Functionalism
- Computational theory of mind

### 5. Ethics

Topics:

- Moral reasoning
- Utilitarianism
- Deontology
- Virtue ethics
- AI ethics
- Responsibility

### 6. Philosophy of Science

Topics:

- Scientific method
- Falsification
- Paradigms
- Explanation
- Probability and evidence
- Cosmological models
